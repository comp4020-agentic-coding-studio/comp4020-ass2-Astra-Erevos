# What knowing a cat does NOT mean

Certainty is not the measure of understanding.

NOT — predict every response

NOT — turn preferences into rules

NOT — eliminate uncertainty

NOT — finish the model

no "case closed" stamp

# What knowing a cat actually means

Knowing your cat includes knowing what you still do not know.

SPECIFIC — This human, not people in general

REVISABLE — Current evidence can change the model

TRACEABLE — You can explain why the model changed

HONEST — Unknown stays unknown when evidence runs out

Know what you know, why you know it, what changed, and what remains open.
