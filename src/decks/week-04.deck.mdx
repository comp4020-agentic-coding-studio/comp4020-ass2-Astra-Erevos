# Four kinds of history

This week asks: what kind of history is this?

RECURRING PATTERN

ONE-OFF EVENT

Something that appears often enough to reasonably expect again.

Something real that happened, but should not automatically be generalised.

SHARED CONVENTION

UNCERTAIN HISTORY

Something whose meaning exists because this human cat and this agent built it together.

Something seen once or twice whose significance is still unresolved.

The past matters differently depending on what kind of past it is.
