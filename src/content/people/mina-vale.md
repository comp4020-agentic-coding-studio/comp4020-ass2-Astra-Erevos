Mina Vale tutors one of the three parallel Care Lab groups running each
week, Weeks 2 to 12. Before this course she spent several semesters
marking observation logs for a field-methods unit two buildings over, an
experience she describes mainly as learning how many different ways a
person can write "obviously" about something they did not check.

Her groups meet on the same weekly rhythm as everyone else's Care Lab and
work through the same task, so a question that comes up in her session is
usually worth raising with your own tutor too rather than emailing her
directly if you are not in her group.
