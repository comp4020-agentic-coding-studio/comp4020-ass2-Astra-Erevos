# WEEK 3 What gives a signal meaning?

Context makes interpretation informed — not certain.

REPETITION

ROUTINE

A phrase or behaviour that recurs.

A signal that appears inside an established pattern.

REGISTER

INTERACTION HISTORY

Tone, phrasing or style whose meaning depends on how this person normally speaks.

The same words can carry different evidence after different shared experiences.

Context can justify a reading. It cannot finish the person.

# WEEK 3 Interpretation must stay revisable

Interpretation is not certainty.

Week 3 makes inference more grounded, not more final.

OBSERVED Mira said / did X.

INTERPRETED Given repetition, routine and history, it may mean Y.

STILL OPEN What evidence would make you revise this reading?

An interpretation is safer when you can explain why you made it — and what would make you change it.
