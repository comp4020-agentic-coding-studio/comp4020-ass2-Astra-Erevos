## Before your Care Lab

**Released Mon 22 Mar 2027 · due Wed 31 Mar 2027, 08:30**, ahead of the
earliest timetabled group.

Take memories about your designated human cat — your Catwatch log, last
week's four history piles, anything you have added since — and sort them
into Week 5's three states: KEEP, HOLD LIGHTLY, LET EXPIRE. Do not stop at
the label. For each entry, write one sentence naming what evidence would move
it: what would demote a KEEP to HOLD LIGHTLY? What would promote a HOLD
LIGHTLY that keeps getting confirmed? What would have to happen for a LET
EXPIRE entry to earn its way back into relevance?

Keep last week's distinction intact while you work: history type and memory
state are different dimensions. A recurring pattern does not automatically
mean KEEP — a pattern you have not seen confirmed in a while may deserve
HOLD LIGHTLY. A one-off event is not automatically LET EXPIRE either, if it
was significant enough that you would still want to know about it.

## In the Care Lab

In your timetabled group, compare your sorting, and pay particular attention
to any entry you moved: what did you first file it as, what changed your
mind, and was the trigger new evidence or just time passing? Time passing on
its own is a weak reason to move a state — the stronger version always
points at something your human cat actually said or did.

Discuss where a KEEP entry felt uncomfortable to downgrade. That discomfort
is worth noticing directly: an entry you are reluctant to move is sometimes
one you are protecting because it is convenient, not because the evidence
still supports it.

## Afterwards

This week's lecture, The Cat Has Changed, is about exactly the harder version
of what you just practised: a KEEP entry that stops being true without ever
having been foolish to trust. Bring your sorted memories into it, and watch
for which of them this lecture makes you want to move again. The Care Model,
due in Week 7 once the teaching break that starts next week has ended, is
where this triage stops being an exercise and starts being the thing you are
graded on getting honest.
