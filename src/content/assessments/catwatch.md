## The brief

> Watch a human-cat interaction closely enough to tell the difference between
> what happened and what you assumed happened.

Catwatch asks you to do the thing Week 1 argues is hardest: observe before
you respond. You are not building a model yet, and you are not fixing
anything. You are training yourself to notice how much of what feels like
"reading" a cat is actually invention — an inference dressed up as a fact.

## What you submit

A short observation log of one ongoing human-cat interaction (real, adapted,
or supplied — see your Care Lab). Every entry is tagged Observed, Inferred,
or Unknown, and every Inferred entry says what it was inferred from. Revisit
the log at least once, and mark anywhere a later look changed an earlier
tag — including inferences you downgrade to unknown.

## Where this goes next

Catwatch is not marked as an isolated skill. What you notice here — which
signals you kept misreading, which "obvious" facts turned out to be
assumptions — is exactly the raw material The Care Model asks you to
structure in Weeks 4 to 7.
