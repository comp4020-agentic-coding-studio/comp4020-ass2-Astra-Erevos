## What a Care Lab is

A Care Lab is where the ideas from lecture meet an actual case. Each one runs
in several small timetabled groups rather than a single whole-class session
— check your enrolment for which group and room is yours. Content is
identical across groups; only the time differs.

Most weeks pair a short task, released the Monday before, with in-session
discussion of what you found. Weeks 4 and 7 are showcases instead: Catwatch
and The Care Model have just been submitted, and the Lab time is spent
presenting and discussing that work rather than starting something new.

## What you submit

Nothing is submitted to this page directly. Each Care Lab's own page names
its weekly task, if it has one, and its due time. Completion of that task, or
participation in that week's showcase or discussion, is what is marked.

## How it's marked

Eleven opportunities, best 10 of 11 counted, each worth 1% toward this 10%
total. Week 12 is reserved as a recovery week for anyone who needs to
replace a result, and as an open clinic for everyone else.
