## No separate task this week

The Care Model is already due — Wed 21 Apr 2027, 08:30, before the earliest
timetabled group — so there is no new preparation task. Come to your Care
Lab having already submitted, with your model in hand and at least one
revision picked out that you can point to directly.

This is the first time the class has met since the two-week teaching break,
and the break is the point: your human cat did not pause for it, so your
model has had two weeks to be tested by whatever actually happened without
you watching.

## In the Care Lab

This week's session is a showcase, not a new exercise: in your timetabled
group, each person shows how their model handled change, uncertainty, the
break itself, and information that no longer deserved the confidence it
started with. Show at least one visible revision — not a silent replacement,
the actual moment the model updated and why.

This should feel less like presenting a finished piece of work and more like
the transition from building a model to living with its consequences.
Listening to where other people's models bent or broke under two weeks of
real contact is often more useful than reviewing your own — the failure
modes that show up across several models at once are usually the ones worth
naming out loud.

## Afterwards

Know Your Cat opens course-wide at 09:00 on Wednesday 21 April, immediately
after The Care Model closes. What you just showed your group — a model that has
already had to revise itself once — is exactly the starting condition Know
Your Cat expects. This week's lecture, When Signals Disagree, gives you the
next piece: what to do the next time your human cat's current message, your
established read of them, and their recent behaviour do not agree.
