# WEEK 2 Observation comes before interpretation

Separate what happened from what you think it meant.

OBSERVED INTERPRETED

what actually happened what you think it may mean

• exact words • visible action • timing • what changed from usual

• possible mood • possible preference • possible reason • how confident you are

Do not let an inference quietly become a fact.
