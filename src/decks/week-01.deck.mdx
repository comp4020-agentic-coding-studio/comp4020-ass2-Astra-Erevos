# Generic support vs personalised care

GENERIC SUPPORT

PERSONALISED CARE

• sounds supportive • follows best practice • works reasonably often • treats support as a property of the response

• fits this person • depends on history and context • stays revisable • treats support as a relationship between response and recipient

The same sentence can be right for one person and quietly wrong for another.
