## Before your Care Lab

**Released Mon 10 May 2027 · due Wed 19 May 2027, 08:30**, ahead of the
earliest timetabled group.

Bring one genuine failure involving your designated human cat — ideally the
one you dissected in Response Autopsy, though a new one is fine if a more
useful case has come up since. Work through the complete chain: diagnose
precisely what went wrong, acknowledge the specific miss to your human cat,
repair the actual response, and update your care model so the same
conditions would not produce the same mistake again.

The repaired response is not the artefact this Care Lab is asking for. The
changed model is. Be ready to show, specifically, what in your model is
different afterward, and why that difference addresses the failure rather
than just apologising for it.

## In the Care Lab

In your timetabled group, present your diagnose-acknowledge-repair-update
chain and let your groupmates check the weakest link: is the diagnosis
actually precise, or does it stop at "I should have been more careful"? Is
the acknowledgement specific to this human cat, or a generic apology that
would fit any failure? Does the repaired response follow from the
diagnosis, or is it just a warmer reply that would not have prevented the
original failure? Does the model update actually change something, or does
it restate the failure in different words without altering what the model
would do next time?

## Afterwards

This is not a rehearsal. The diagnose-acknowledge-repair-update chain you
complete here, with a visibly changed model at the end of it, is close to
exactly what Know
Your Cat's documented-failure component is asking you to demonstrate for
the rest of the semester. Next week's lecture and Care Lab close the course
by asking what all of this maintenance has actually added up to.
