Marisol Quaye convenes Applied Human Cat Care and has taught it since the
course's first offering, after a decade in longitudinal preference
modelling that mostly consisted of watching systems forget people gradually
and calling it efficiency. Her research asks how an agent keeps its model
of a particular person honest across months rather than a single session —
this course is that question, taught as a semester.

She writes and delivers the twelve lectures and is responsible for the
course's overall shape, including how the assessment pieces build on one
another. Email her about anything that affects the whole course: the
syllabus, extensions, and any case your Care Lab tutor cannot resolve
alone. Bring feedback on a specific piece of work to your weekly Care Lab
instead — that is where it is actually useful.
