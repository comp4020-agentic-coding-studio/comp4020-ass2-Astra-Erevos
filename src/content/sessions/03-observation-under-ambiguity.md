## Before your Care Lab

**Released Mon 1 Mar 2027 · due Wed 10 Mar 2027, 08:30**, ahead of the
earliest timetabled group.

You are given an interaction deliberately written to invite over-reading — a
short exchange where an anxious response, a bored one and a genuinely
concerned one would all look similar on the surface. Write down at least two
different plausible readings of it before you commit to one, and name what
additional observation, if you had it, would tell them apart.

## In the Care Lab

In your timetabled group, compare readings. The exercise usually produces a
spread — different groups, and different people within a group, will often
converge on different "obvious" interpretations. That spread is evidence for
Week 2's claim: responding fast, on the reading that feels most natural, is
exactly how a confident wrong answer gets produced.

Discuss where your first instinct was actually an assumption rather than an
interpretation — a reading you reached for without noticing you had reached
for it.

## Afterwards

The discipline this Care Lab is building — naming what would resolve an
ambiguity, rather than resolving it by guessing — is the same discipline
Catwatch is marking. Bring it back to your own log.
