## Weekly rhythm

Care Labs run as eleven weekly opportunities, Weeks 2 through 12 — Week 1 is
lecture only, so there is nothing to bring to a Care Lab before you have
watched anything to log. Each ordinary week's task is released the previous
Monday and due by Wednesday 08:30, ahead of that week's earliest timetabled
group; several small groups run from Wednesday onward, at different times
but with identical content, so check your enrolment for which one is yours.

The date shown at the top of this page is the Week 12 Wednesday recovery
point — the last of the eleven opportunities, not a single deadline for the
whole stream. Each ordinary week keeps its own release-and-due rhythm, named
on that week's own Care Lab page.

Two weeks break the ordinary rhythm on purpose. Weeks 4 and 7 are showcases
rather than fresh preparation tasks: Catwatch and The Care Model have just
been submitted, and the Lab time is spent presenting that work instead of
starting something new. Week 12 is the recovery opportunity — complete an
equivalent case there to replace a missing or weak week, or use the session
as an open capstone clinic if your other ten results already satisfy you.

## What a Care Lab is

A Care Lab is where the ideas from lecture meet an actual human-cat case,
worked through with your timetabled group rather than alone.

## What you hand in

Nothing is submitted to this page directly. Each Care Lab's own page names
its weekly task, if it has one, and its due time. Completion of that task,
or participation in that week's showcase or clinic, is what counts.
