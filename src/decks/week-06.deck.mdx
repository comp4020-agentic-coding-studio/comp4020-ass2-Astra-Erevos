# What can change?

Week 6 is not about bad memory. It is about the conditions under which an old, reasonable model begins to age.

PREFERENCE — what helps — quiet first can become conversation first

ROUTINE — when it happens — a post-work ritual can disappear or move

SIGNAL — what it means — the same phrase can carry a new request

CIRCUMSTANCE — why it matters — a new job can change the old preference

not contradiction: continuation over time

No single dramatic reversal. Just repeated interactions that make the old model harder to trust.

# When a good rule becomes too rigid

A response can fail precisely because it worked very well before.

WORKED BEFORE — The old success: The agent gives Mira space after work. Mira decompresses, then returns later. Appropriate for many earlier days.

FAILURE NOW — The mechanical replay: Agent: "I'll stay quiet and give you space." Mira: "I was hoping you'd ask me about it." Personalised care failed by over-trusting itself.

remembered yesterday too confidently

# Change, exception, uncertainty, or rigidity?

These are not four buttons. They are competing explanations to keep alive while evidence accumulates.

Change — Is this becoming a new pattern?

Exception — Is this a contextual deviation?

Uncertainty — Is the evidence still too thin?

Rigidity — Was the old rule written too absolutely?

SAME EVIDENCE: longer debriefs, more uptake, disappointment at silence

review next 3 interactions
